## Gaps (NH does not state these, so leave them blank)
- **Adult compression:ventilation ratio** is not given as 30:2. 3.2A/3.6 say only "BVM ventilation 1 breath every 10 chest compressions without interrupting compressions" (or passive O2 by NRB for the first 8 min).
- **Adult ventilation rate with an advanced airway**: not stated in 3.2A. (3.4 post-ROSC: 10-12/min adult, 20/min peds.)
- **Pediatric compression rate and depth**: not stated in 3.2P (it says only "use metronome if possible"). 3.6 (rate 100-<120, depth 2-2.4 in) is titled "Team Focused CPR-Adult".
- **Maximum pause in seconds**: no number anywhere. The rule is to interrupt only for rhythm/pulse check and defibrillation.
- **Adult AED/manual energy**: only "at the device's maximum energy". No joule value.
- **Adult anti-dysrhythmic dose and timing**: "per ACLS algorithms" only. No NH dose in 3.2A.
- **Pediatric epinephrine for asystole/PEA**: no first-dose timing, only "may repeat every other cycle". For VF/VT, the first dose comes after the 2nd shock.
- **Pediatric team-CPR protocol**: none. 3.6 covers adults only.

## Conflicts to flag
- **Compressor rotation**: 3.6 p.94 says compressors alternate every 1 minute ("Calls for compressor change every one minute"). 3.6 p.95 says "alternating after 2 minutes", and its checklist says "rotated at minimum every 2 minutes". 3.2A/3.2P pearls say "at least every two minutes".
- **Extended-resuscitation clock**: the 20-minute TOR minimum counts from *arrival on scene* (8.15 p.216). The 60-minute extended-resuscitation limit counts from *time of dispatch* (3.2A p.85, 8.15 p.217).
- **3.4** is the combined "Post Resuscitative Care Adult & Pediatric" (p.90). 3.2P refers to it as "3.4P".
- The Traumatic Cardiac Arrest protocol (4.10) is out of scope and not extracted, apart from noting that it says epinephrine is not recommended.
