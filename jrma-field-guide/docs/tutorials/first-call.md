# Tutorial: run a practice call

Do this once at home before using the app on a real call.

1. `npm run serve`, open http://localhost:8080 (or the hosted URL on your phone).
2. **Call tab.** Tap *On scene*, then *Patient contact*. Each tap stamps the time. The top clock counts from patient contact.
3. Enter age 64, sex Male, chief complaint "chest pressure". Tap outside the box. An *Open: Chest Pain* button appears.
4. Tap **Start primary survey** first. Answer each card. Red boxes under the top bar are red flags.
5. Open **Chest Pain**. Read the italic line to the patient. Tap the answer.
6. Action cards (aspirin, oxygen) have three buttons: *Done*, *Not done*, *Contraindicated / refused*. Pick one; it is time-stamped.
7. **Vitals tab.** Enter a set, tap Save. The *Reassess* timer at the top starts (15 min stable, 5 min unstable, set on the Call tab). Out-of-range adult values turn orange (abnormal) or red (critical).
8. **History tab.** Fill SAMPLE and OPQRST as you get answers.
9. **Report tab.** Read the MIST report to the RN, or tap *Copy report text* and paste into your ePCR narrative. *Check before handoff* lists critical steps on your path that were not done.
10. **Call tab → End call and erase data.** This wipes the call from the phone.
